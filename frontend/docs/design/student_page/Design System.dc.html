01 — Foundations

Color

Livingstone blue is the identity color, not the interface color. At 1.9:1 against white it cannot legally carry text or a primary button — so it fills, tints, charts and shapes, while a deepened blue-gray drawn from it does the work that needs contrast.

Livingstone Blue#8CB0BF

Official blue. Fills, tints, progress tracks, decorative shapes, photo washes. Never text, never a button fill.

Livingstone Slate#2E4A57

The blue darkened until it works. Primary buttons, links, focus rings, active nav. 8.9:1 on paper.

Black#000000

All body and heading text. The trademark's default weight on light grounds.

Deep Band#101B20

Section layering: hero band, support CTA, footer. Blue-cast near-black, never pure black at scale.

Three ramps on one perceptual lightness scale — the same step of any role carries the same visual weight. Tints come from 100–300, bases from 500, text-on-tint and pressed states from 700–900.

Space

A 1.10× scale: 4 · 9 · 13 · 18 · 26 · 35, then 56 / 88 for section rhythm. Sections breathe at 88; cards at 26.

Radius

8 for chips and swatches, 16 for panels, 28+ for cards and dialogs. Buttons, inputs and tags go fully pill. No sharp corners anywhere.

Elevation & motion

Three shadows, ink-tinted to the warm ground. Motion is 160ms ease-out for state, 240ms for panels; progress bars fill once on load; prefers-reduced-motion drops all of it.

05 — Responsive

Three breakpoints

Most students will meet this portal on a phone, in a hallway, deciding whether they still have to walk somewhere. Mobile is the primary case, not the fallback.

Mobile · 390

Single column. Sidebar becomes a bottom tab bar; the status header collapses to ring + next action; the tracker is a stacked list. Continue Registration pins to the bottom above the tabs. 44px targets.

Tablet · 834

Two columns. Sidebar collapses to a 72px icon rail; the tracker keeps full rows; alerts and activity stack beneath the tracker rather than beside it.

Desktop · 1440

Full 260px sidebar, 1180px content field, tracker and rail side by side. Landing sections run to a 1180px measure with copy capped at 66ch.
